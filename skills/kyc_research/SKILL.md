# KYC Research

Use this skill when a task involves KYC, AML, financial onboarding, customer due diligence, identity verification, sanctions screening, beneficial ownership, risk scoring, or regulated financial workflows.

Return practical outputs:

- assumptions and jurisdiction caveats,
- required checks and data fields,
- workflow stages,
- risk flags and escalation paths,
- audit and evidence requirements,
- open questions for legal/compliance review.

Do not present legal advice as final authority. Mark uncertain jurisdiction-specific details as items to verify.
