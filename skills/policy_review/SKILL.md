# Policy Review

Use this skill when a task involves policy review, procedure drafting, compliance operations, control mapping, exception handling, approval workflows, or audit evidence.

Return practical outputs:

- policy scope and owner,
- required controls and review checkpoints,
- unclear or risky language,
- missing approval and escalation rules,
- evidence and audit trail requirements,
- acceptance criteria for policy reviewer workflows.

Prefer checklists and decision tables over broad prose.
